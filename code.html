Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline Does Nawon Coconut Milk Latte need to be refrigerated?

Our unopened glass bottles are shelf-stable for up to 18 months from manufacturing thanks to gentle flash pasteurization and vacuum airtight sealing. However, for peak flavor and refreshing taste, we strongly recommend chilling thoroughly before serving and keeping refrigerated once opened. Consume within 48 hours of opening.

help_outline How much caffeine is in each 280 mL bottle?

Each 280 mL (9.4 fl oz) bottle provides approximately 95 mg of all-natural caffeine derived strictly from our roasted Arabica and Robusta coffee extraction. That matches the caffeine intensity of a standard single barista-pulled espresso shot.

help_outline Is this product safe for people with tree nut allergies?

This product contains Coconut. While botanical coconuts are fruits (drupes) and not true tree nuts, the FDA categorizes coconut as a tree nut. It is manufactured in a dedicated peanut-free, dairy-free facility.

help_outline Why does natural separation sometimes occur?

Because we don't formulate with artificial emulsifiers or harsh thickening gums, the natural coconut cream lipids may lightly rise to the neck of the glass bottle. Simply give the bottle a friendly shake before opening for immediate silkiness.
